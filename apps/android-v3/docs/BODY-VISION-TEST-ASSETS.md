# Body vision test assets

The two small images under `app/src/androidTest/assets` are test-only fixtures
derived from the Apache-2.0 licensed Google AI Edge MediaPipe samples. They are
not packaged in the AIYA application APK.

- `face.jpg`: resized JPEG derived from
  `examples/face_landmarker/ios/FaceLandmarkerTests/business-person.png`
- `hand_thumb_up.jpg`: copied from
  `examples/gesture_recognizer/android/app/src/androidTest/assets/hand_thumb_up.jpg`

Upstream project: <https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe-samples>
